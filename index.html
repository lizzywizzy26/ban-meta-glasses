Evidence

What's actually been documented (BBC, 2026)

  • A London woman ("Alice") was covertly filmed via smart glasses in a shopping centre; the video reached ~40,000 views, and when she asked for it to be removed, the man behind it said he'd only take it down as a "paid service."
  • A separate BBC investigation identified nearly 50 women filmed without their knowledge on nights out, across 65+ online channels with 3bn+ combined views.
  • Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood said this misuse of technology "will not be tolerated." Lib Dem MP Wera Hobhouse has introduced a bill to close the legal loophole around covert filming.
  • A March 2026 poll found 49% of Britons support banning smart glasses in public spaces (30% opposed).

Source: BBC News, "Woman covertly filmed for 'humiliating' social media content – then told to pay" (7 May 2026) and "Men covertly filming women at night and profiting from footage" (Feb 2026).

D

Write to your MP

A petition asks the government to act. Your MP can act directly — Liberal Democrat MP Wera Hobhouse has already introduced a private member's bill to close the legal loophole around covert filming and force platforms to remove this content fast. A short email from a constituent carries real weight.

Ray-Ban is owned by EssilorLuxottica, and it's their brand name on the "Ray-Ban Meta" glasses — they chose this partnership and can end it. A direct message adds pressure alongside the petition and your MP.
